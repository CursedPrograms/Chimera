Chimera

A layer-based image editor: layers, masks, blend modes, selections, adjustments.

Get it on GitHub How to build

Layers and masks

Unlimited layers with their own size and position. True layer masks: black hides, white reveals. Paint them, blur them, curve them, switch them off with Shift-click, see them with Alt-click. On a mask the brush paints black and white.

27 blend modes

Every Photoshop layer mode, from Multiply and Screen to Vivid Light, Hard Mix, Hue and Luminosity, with the same maths.

Selections

Marquees, Lasso, Polygonal Lasso and Magic Wand, with add, subtract and intersect, feathering and marching ants. Select Subject and Remove Background find the subject for you, on your own PC, and Select and Mask gives it real hair: edges that follow the picture, feather, shift edge and colour decontamination.

Painting

A brush and eraser with size, hardness and opacity; a paint bucket with tolerance; an eyedropper. Fill an empty layer, set its blend mode, mask it.

Adjustments

Photoshop's 15 adjustments as adjustment layers that stay editable: Curves, Levels, Exposure, Hue/Saturation, Color Balance, Black & White, Photo Filter, Channel Mixer, Gradient Map, Selective Color and more, each with its own mask, in a Properties panel.

Filters and size

Gaussian Blur, Box Blur, Average and Add Noise. Free Transform, Image Size (bicubic) and Canvas Size. PNG with transparency, JPG, and projects that keep every layer.

Changeling filters

With Changeling Studio on your PC, Filter > Changeling runs Imago, Tiresias, Chronos, Natio, Cosmeta and Tonsor on the picture. Each result comes in as a new layer, masked to the selection.

Depth maps

With Bathos on your PC, Filter > Bathos > Depth Map puts the depth of the picture in as a new layer: near is bright, far is dark, and faces get a real 3D shape.

Face swap

With Mimic on your PC, Filter > Mimic swaps a face from a photo onto the picture, as a new layer you can mask.

The usual shortcuts
